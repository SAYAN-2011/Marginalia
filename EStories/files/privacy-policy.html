Privacy Policy

How this site handles information

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Firelight Tales is a small reading site. This page explains, plainly, what happens to information when you use it — both as a reader and, if you have the password, as the site's admin.

The short version

Reading the site itself doesn't require an account and isn't tracked by us directly. This site does, however, show ads served by Google and/or other advertising partners, and those partners use cookies and similar technology to do things like measure ad performance and, in some cases, personalize the ads you see. The rest of this page explains that in more detail, along with how you can opt out.

Information we collect from readers

We don't run our own analytics or tracking scripts, and there's no sign-up, comments, or forms on this site. We — the people who made this site — don't personally collect or store any information about readers.

However, this site displays advertising, which is served by Google and/or other third-party advertising companies. Those companies operate independently of us and may collect information as described below.

Cookies

We don't set our own cookies. The advertising partners on this site do use cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons or device identifiers) — see the Advertising section below.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google and/or other third-party advertising companies ("ad partners"). These are separate companies from us, with their own privacy practices, and this policy doesn't cover what they do with data once it's in their hands — you'll need to check each partner's own privacy policy for that.

In general, ad partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to:

  • Serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet (sometimes called interest-based or personalized advertising).
  • Measure how well an ad performed, and limit how many times you see the same one.
  • Help prevent fraudulent or abusive activity on the advertising system.

Google, specifically, may use advertising cookies (including the Google Ads/DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by Google by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices or networkadvertising.org/choices.

You can also control cookies more broadly through your browser's own settings, including blocking or deleting them — though doing so may affect ads and other content on this and other sites.

Browser storage (used by the admin panel)

The admin page (admin.html) lets whoever is signed in add, edit, and remove stories. Those changes are saved using your browser's own local storage — either the browser's built-in localStorage, or, if this page is being viewed inside Claude.ai's own preview, Anthropic's equivalent storage for that preview.

  • This data stays on the device making the change. It is not transmitted to us, to Anthropic, or to any third party.
  • It's only used to let the admin preview edits before publishing them (via the "Export JSON" feature, which downloads an updated stories.json file).
  • You can clear it at any time through your browser's own settings (clearing site data / local storage for this page), or by using the "Reset demo data" button in the admin panel.

The admin password

The admin sign-in is a simple client-side password check, included for demonstration purposes only. It does not protect real content or real user data, and no login information is collected, stored remotely, or transmitted anywhere — the check happens entirely in your browser.

Third-party services

This site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Loading a font causes your browser to make a request directly to Google's servers, which may log standard technical information such as your IP address, as described in Google's own privacy policy. We don't control or receive that data ourselves.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone ourselves. Our ad partners are expected to serve ads in line with applicable children's privacy laws, but we don't control how they detect or handle this, since ad serving happens on their end, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If how this site handles information ever changes, this page will be updated to reflect that, along with the date at the top.

Questions

This is a small, self-hosted project without a formal support channel. If you're running your own copy of this site, feel free to adapt this policy to describe your own setup accurately.
